# COMPREHENSIVE SALES ANALYSIS (EXCEL)

This project aims to optimize the sales management of the commercial department through data analysis from four internal tables, focusing on three key metrics: total revenue, products sold, and number of closed sales, in order to generate insights that enhance decision-making.

# Project Objectives
### Project Objectives:
1. **Analyze Sales Performance**: Measure the revenue generated by the main clients and the products sold by business line.
   
2. **Evaluate Employee Performance**: Identify the sales closed by each salesperson and understand which employees contribute the most to overall sales.

3. **Optimize Sales Strategy**: Evaluate the best-selling products and identify areas of improvement in sales to adjust marketing and sales strategies.

4. **Generate Key Insights**: Obtain a clear view of the sales trends from 2017-2019 to make strategic decisions.
