# COMPREHENSIVE SALES ANALYSIS (EXCEL)

This project aims to optimize the sales management of the commercial department through data analysis from four internal tables, focusing on three key metrics: total revenue, products sold, and number of closed sales, in order to generate insights that enhance decision-making.

# Project Objectives👩‍💻:
1. **Analyze Sales Performance**: Measure the revenue generated by the main clients and the products sold by business line.
   
2. **Evaluate Employee Performance**: Identify the sales closed by each salesperson and understand which employees contribute the most to overall sales.

3. **Optimize Sales Strategy**: Evaluate the best-selling products and identify areas of improvement in sales to adjust marketing and sales strategies.

4. **Generate Key Insights**: Obtain a clear view of the sales trends from 2017-2019 to make strategic decisions.

# Results:
1. **Total Revenue**: The total revenue generated between 2017 and 2019 is S/ 29,237,104.27.

2. **Products Sold**: A total of 122,384 products were sold.

3. **Sales Closed by Employee**: The number of closed sales varies significantly between employees, with Katty Pérez leading with 5,352 sales, and Karina Alva with 549 sales.

4. **Products Sold Distribution**: Products sold in the corporate line represent 89% of total sales, while the rest corresponds to individual sales (11%).

# Conclusion:
1. **Overall Performance**: The total revenue is high, reflecting strong business performance. Sales are heavily influenced by the corporate line, suggesting that most clients are companies, not individual consumers.

2. **Employee Performance**: Some employees show significantly higher performance, which may indicate differences in their ability to close sales or the type of clients they work with.

3. **Key Clients**: The clients generating the most revenue include large corporations such as British American Tobacco and Banco de Crédito del Perú, showing a concentration of sales with a few high-value clients.

4. **Opportunities**: The small percentage of sales in the individual product line (11%) suggests an opportunity to focus more on this segment and improve sales in this area.

# Learning:
1. **Importance of Segmentation**: The project demonstrates how dividing clients into different segments (corporate vs. individual) provides a clearer view of which strategies should be implemented in each area.

2. **Variable Employee Performance**: Differences in the number of sales closed by employees highlight the importance of training, motivation, and proper resource allocation.

3. **Value of Visual Data**: The charts and tables in the dashboard help identify patterns quickly, making decision-making easier.

# Next Steps:
1. **Adjust Sales Strategies**: Focus more on the individual product line, which has accounted for only 11% of total sales.

2. **Train and Support Underperforming Employees**: Investigate the reasons behind the lower sales of some employees and provide them with the necessary tools to improve.

3. **Expand Client Base**: Seek new business opportunities in sectors beyond the established ones, such as increasing sales of individual products.

4. **Monitor Performance Annually**: Continue measuring results in the coming years to ensure that the implemented strategies are working.
